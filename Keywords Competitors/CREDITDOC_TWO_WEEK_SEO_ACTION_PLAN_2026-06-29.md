# CreditDoc Two-Week SEO Action Plan

Created: 2026-06-29  
Planning window: 2026-06-29 to 2026-07-13  
Context: CreditDoc is roughly 4 months old. Early traffic volatility is expected, but indexing, crawl clarity, exact-intent page coverage, and weekly monitoring must stay tight.

## Strategic Goal

Use the existing content engine, tools, money pages, course, quizzes, answers, and competitor keyword research to build measurable search traction. The next two weeks should not be about producing random pages. The work should focus on:

- getting priority money/tool/course/quiz URLs discovered and indexed;
- filling keyword-backed gaps from competitor research;
- strengthening internal links between money pages, tools, answers, course, and quizzes;
- monitoring GSC and SE Ranking weekly so we refine from data instead of guessing;
- avoiding overreaction to normal young-site volatility unless Google reports a real indexing, crawl, or canonical problem.

## Two-Week Execution Plan

### Day 1 - Baseline And Priority Tracking

Tasks:

- Record current GSC baseline for priority URL groups: clicks, impressions, average position, CTR, indexed status where available.
- Record current SE Ranking issue counts and keyword positions for the tracked competitor keyword list.
- Confirm the new calculator URLs are in the live sitemap after deployment:
  - `/tools/commercial-loan-calculator/`
  - `/tools/business-line-of-credit-calculator/`
- Add these two URLs to the next available indexing/inspection workflow as priority URLs.
- Confirm the tools hub links to every business funding calculator and quiz.

Output:

- One baseline snapshot with date, source, and URL group.

### Day 2 - Indexation Review

Tasks:

- Inspect priority tool, course, quiz, and money URLs in GSC/API where possible.
- Separate pages into:
  - indexed;
  - discovered but not indexed;
  - crawled but not indexed;
  - unknown to Google;
  - canonical mismatch;
  - blocked/noindex/robots issue.
- For unknown/discovered-but-not-indexed priority URLs, schedule them for next indexing submission if the automation supports it.
- Confirm no important tools/course/quiz pages are orphaned.

Output:

- Priority indexation list with action per URL.

### Day 3 - Internal Link Pass: Business Funding

Tasks:

- Add contextual links from business money pages to relevant calculators:
  - business line of credit page to line-of-credit calculator;
  - small business loans page to business loan and commercial loan calculators;
  - SBA page to SBA calculator and SBA guarantee fee calculator;
  - startup business loans page to readiness quiz and line-of-credit calculator;
  - equipment financing page to equipment financing calculator;
  - invoice factoring page to accounts receivable calculator.
- Add reverse links from calculators back to matching money pages and answer pages where missing.

Output:

- Internal link diff and validation build.

### Day 6 - Technical And SERP Quality Check

Tasks:

Output:

### Day 7 - Week 1 Review

Tasks:

- Pull GSC page/query data for priority groups.
- Compare against Day 1 baseline.
- Review SE Ranking issue delta.
- Identify:
  - pages indexed this week;
  - pages still unknown/not indexed;
  - pages with impressions but poor CTR;
  - pages with impressions but wrong query intent;
  - pages needing more internal links.

Decision rules:

- If impressions appear but CTR is weak, adjust title/meta.
- If indexed but no impressions, add more internal links and supporting answers.
- If not indexed and live/sitemap/canonical are correct, keep in submission/inspection cycle and do not rewrite blindly.

Output:

- Week 1 monitoring note and Week 2 refinements.

## Monitoring Cadence

Daily:

- Confirm automation ran.
- Check new critical errors from audits.
- Check priority pages remain live.

Twice weekly:

- GSC indexation checks for priority URLs.
- Sitemap/robots sanity.
- SE Ranking issue delta review.

Weekly:

- GSC page/query export.
- SE Ranking keyword movement.
- Money/tool/course/quiz indexation report.
- Commit and memory update.

## KPI Targets For The Two Weeks

Primary:

- Priority URLs live, sitemap-present, and internally linked.
- No critical robots/noindex/canonical issues on money/tool/course/quiz pages.
- New exact-intent calculator URLs submitted or scheduled for inspection/indexing workflow.
- At least one weekly GSC baseline and one weekly comparison.

Secondary:

- New impressions on calculator/tool/support URLs.
- More queries mapped to correct URL intent.
- Reduced duplicate meta/title issues.
- Fewer important URLs unknown to Google.

Do not judge the sprint only by clicks. At this domain age, discovery, indexation, impressions, and query alignment are valid early wins.

## Decision Rules

- Do not assume a page is bad just because it is not ranking yet.
- Do not rewrite working pages without GSC or SE Ranking evidence.
- Prioritize exact-intent gaps backed by competitor keyword files.
- Prioritize internal links when a page is indexed but quiet.
- Prioritize title/meta changes when a page has impressions but weak CTR.
- Prioritize technical fixes immediately only when Google sees a real crawl, canonical, noindex, robots, or server problem.
- Keep pawn/title/check-cashing work as background moat unless keyword data says otherwise.

CreditDoc is about 4 months old. For new pages and new clusters:

The two-week goal is to tighten the machine: ship exact-intent pages, confirm discovery, monitor query alignment, and refine from evidence.
