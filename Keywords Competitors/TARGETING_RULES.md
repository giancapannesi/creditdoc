# CreditDoc Competitor Keyword Targeting Rules

Saved: 2026-06-22

## Active Targets

Use competitor keyword exports to find opportunities in these categories only:

- Loans
- SBA loans
- Credit cards / cards
- Credit repair

## Commercial Priority

Loans, credit cards, and SBA/business loans are primary user actions.

Credit repair is a derivative/peripheral topic within the lending journey. It is not a primary commercial action for CreditDoc.

Prioritize keyword opportunities in this order:

1. Loan origination
2. SBA loans and business loans
3. Credit cards / cards
4. Credit repair

Credit repair should support the broader lending/card funnel when useful, but it should not dominate the content plan or displace primary loan/card/SBA opportunities.

## Bad-Credit Loan Funnel Framing

Credit repair origination is low-value unless it is attached to high-volume lending intent.

The valuable user is usually not "shopping for credit repair" as the primary action. The valuable user is trying to get a loan or card, but bad credit affects approval odds, APR, limits, or terms.

Treat these as primary opportunities:

- `personal loans for bad credit`
- `bad credit loans`
- `installment loans for bad credit`
- `emergency loans for bad credit`
- `business loans for bad credit`
- `SBA loans with bad credit`
- `credit cards for bad credit`
- `secured credit cards`
- `credit builder cards`
- `loan denied because of credit score`
- `how credit score affects loan interest rate`

Use credit repair as a secondary path when the user is not yet loan/card-ready or when improving credit could materially improve loan pricing.

Examples of acceptable credit-repair usage:

- Explaining how credit repair affects loan approval odds
- Linking from credit-score or dispute content into loans/cards where appropriate
- Supporting users who are not yet loan/card-ready
- Explaining how credit score affects APR, approval odds, limits, collateral, and co-signer requirements

Examples of lower-priority credit-repair usage:

- Standalone credit-repair company pages
- Competitor-brand credit-repair pages
- Broad credit-repair content that does not connect back to loans, cards, or SBA/business financing

## Excluded For Now

Do not prioritize or build pages for:

- Insurance
- Car insurance
- Home insurance
- General money transfer
- Broad non-credit personal finance topics outside the active targets

## Competitor Brand Terms

Do not lead with competitor-brand targeting such as Credit Saint, Lexington Law, or similar brand-name keywords.

Competitor names may be used only as natural comparison context inside broader category pages, not as the main SEO target.

## Preferred Keyword Intent

Prioritize:

- Category terms, such as `best credit repair companies`
- Problem terms, such as `remove collections from credit report`
- Product terms, such as `bad credit personal loans`, `business loans`, `SBA loans`, `secured credit cards`
- Comparison terms where the page is genuinely useful and category-led
