# CreditDoc Master Loan, Card, And SBA Ranking Plan

Saved: 2026-06-22

## Objective

Build CreditDoc's organic growth around high-value primary actions:

1. Loan origination
2. SBA and business financing
3. Credit cards / secured cards / credit-builder cards

Credit repair is not a primary action. It is a supporting/peripheral topic that helps users become ready for loans or cards, or helps explain why bad credit affects approval, APR, limits, collateral, and co-signer requirements.

## Strategic Rules

### Include

- Personal loans
- Bad-credit loans
- Business loans
- SBA loans
- Startup business loans
- Working capital loans
- Equipment financing
- Business lines of credit
- Accounts receivable financing
- Credit cards
- Secured cards
- Credit-builder cards
- Loan/card readiness topics

### Exclude For Now

- Insurance
- Car insurance
- Home insurance
- General money transfer
- Broad finance topics that do not connect to loans, SBA/business financing, or cards
- Competitor-brand-led pages

### Competitor Brand Terms

Do not lead with competitor-brand targeting.

Competitor names may appear naturally inside comparison tables or contextual sections, but they should not define the SEO strategy.

## Core Thesis

Google should already understand CreditDoc as a credit and consumer-finance site. The next step is to concentrate topical authority around commercial lending/card clusters.

Competitor research so far shows that successful loan competitors rank with:

1. Commercial money pages
2. Calculators and tools
3. Eligibility/friction answer pages
4. Supporting guides

CreditDoc should use the same structure, but every page must map into a cluster and a money page.

## Cluster Architecture

Each cluster must have:

- Primary money page
- Supporting answer pages
- Supporting calculator/tool
- Related category page
- Internal links into the money page
- Internal links back out from the money page
- GSC/indexing monitoring

Preferred link flow:

```text
supporting answer -> supporting tool -> primary money page
primary money page -> supporting answers/tools
category page -> primary money page
```

Do not create standalone informational pages unless they support a money page.

## Findings From Current Competitor Files

### BestMoney

BestMoney is broad and noisy. It has card and mortgage signal, but also many excluded topics such as insurance and money transfer.

Useful pattern:

- Card comparison pages
- Card review pages
- Card guide pages
- Mortgage/loan comparison pages

Use it mainly for page-type clues, not broad strategy.

### Credit Saint

Credit Saint is mostly credit repair and brand traffic.

Useful signal:

- Bad-credit and approval-friction language
- Credit repair as a loan/card readiness support topic

Do not build the strategy around Credit Saint or credit repair as a standalone funnel.

### Lendio

Lendio is the strongest file so far for the current strategy.

Filtered Lendio findings:

- 455 loan/SBA/business/card rows kept from 1,000
- Business loans: 316 keywords, 435,650 volume, avg KD 50.6
- SBA loans: 88 keywords, 106,240 volume, avg KD 55.0
- Calculators/tools: 79 keywords, 54,630 volume, avg KD 35.5
- Bad-credit/startup friction: 79 keywords, 99,650 volume, avg KD 46.8

Important Lendio pattern:

- They rank with calculators.
- They rank with business-loan category pages.
- They rank with friction guides such as bad credit, startup, and requirements pages.

This supports a tool + money page + answer cluster strategy.

## Money Page Requirements

Money pages must be stronger than thin listicles.

Required sections:

- Product definition
- Best-fit borrower profiles
- Qualification requirements
- Credit-score expectations
- Revenue/time-in-business requirements
- Collateral/personal guarantee notes
- Cost and fee ranges
- Time to funding
- Comparison table
- Risks and unsuitable borrowers
- Alternatives
- Readiness checklist
- FAQ
- Links to tools
- Links to supporting answers

Business loan money pages should compare:

- SBA loans
- Term loans
- Business lines of credit
- Equipment financing
- Invoice factoring / accounts receivable financing
- Merchant cash advances
- Secured business loans
- Personal loans used for business, with caution

## Tool Requirements

Each calculator/tool should be useful above the fold and support commercial pages.

Required structure:

- Calculator first
- Inputs that match real borrower questions
- Monthly payment / cost output where relevant
- Qualification notes
- Plain-English explanation
- Related loan products
- Link to the relevant money page
- FAQ

Priority tools:

1. SBA loan calculator
2. SBA guarantee fee calculator
3. Business loan payment calculator
4. Accounts receivable financing calculator
5. Equipment financing calculator
6. Working capital calculator

## Supporting Answer Requirements

Answer pages should capture long-tail questions and push users into money pages.

Required structure:

- Direct answer at top
- Who it applies to
- Credit/revenue/time-in-business requirements
- Options available
- Risks and alternatives
- Link to relevant tool
- Link to primary money page
- FAQ

Examples:

- Can you get a business loan with bad credit?
- What credit score do you need for a business loan?
- Can you get an SBA loan with bad credit?
- What documents do you need for a business loan?
- Why was my business loan denied?
- Can a startup get a business line of credit?

## Decision Rules

Create a new page only if:

- The keyword maps to a primary commercial action.
- The page has a clear cluster and money-page role.
- The intent is not already covered cleanly.
- The term has useful volume, CPC, or strategic value.

Improve an existing page if:

- It already matches the intent.
- It can be made the cluster's primary money page or support page.
- It is already indexed or has impressions.

Ignore a keyword if:

- It is insurance or non-target finance.
- It is competitor-brand-led.
- It is too broad and not tied to a loan/card/SBA action.
- It would create isolated content with no money-page path.
