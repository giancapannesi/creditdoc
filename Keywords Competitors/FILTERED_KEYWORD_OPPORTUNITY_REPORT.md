## Practical Targeting Notes

- `bestmoney.com` mostly targets cards and loan/card review/list pages in this filtered set. Many card terms are high volume but very hard, so CreditDoc should prefer bad-credit/secured/card-readiness variants over broad travel/reward-card head terms.
- `creditsaint.com` contributes fewer primary loan/card terms after brand and pure credit-repair filtering. Its useful signal is bad-credit/approval/friction language, not Credit Saint brand traffic.
- For CreditDoc, the cleanest page types are `/best/` money pages for commercial lists, `/answers/` for approval/APR/friction questions, `/tools/` for readiness calculators/quizzes, and `/review/` only where the provider is directly relevant to loans/cards/SBA.
- Avoid building pages only because a competitor ranks. Build only where the user can take a primary action: apply for a loan, compare cards, assess SBA/business financing, or improve readiness for those actions.
