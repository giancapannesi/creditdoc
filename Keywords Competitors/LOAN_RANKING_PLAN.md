# CreditDoc Loan Ranking Plan

Saved: 2026-06-22

## Strategic Position

Google should already have enough CreditDoc context to understand the site as a credit, lending, credit-card, and consumer-finance resource. The next move is not broad topic expansion. The next move is to concentrate authority around loan-origination pages with supporting calculators and eligibility content.

Primary commercial actions:

1. Business loans
2. SBA loans
3. Personal loans / bad-credit loans
4. Credit cards / secured cards / credit-builder cards

Credit repair remains peripheral. It should support loan/card readiness, not become the main funnel.

## Ranking Thesis

The Lendio export shows that loan competitors are ranking through three repeatable page types:

1. Calculator/tool pages
2. Commercial loan category pages
3. Eligibility/friction answer pages

CreditDoc should use the same structure:

- Tools capture calculator intent and earn links/internal authority.
- Commercial hubs capture high-value comparison/apply intent.
- Answer pages capture long-tail approval, bad-credit, startup, and documentation questions.

## Cluster-To-Money-Page Model

Every keyword cluster must have a defined money page. Supporting answers and tools exist to push authority and qualified users into that page.

Do not create standalone informational pages unless they belong to a cluster with a money page.

Required mapping for each cluster:

- Primary money page
- Supporting answer pages
- Supporting tool/calculator
- Related category page
- Internal links into and out of the cluster
- Indexing/monitoring target

Working model:

| Cluster | Primary Money Page | Supporting Tool | Supporting Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bad-credit business loans | `/best/business-loans-for-bad-credit/` | `/tools/business-loan-readiness-quiz/` | credit score requirements, denial reasons, startup/no revenue, documents needed |
| SBA loans | `/best/sba-loans/` | `/tools/sba-loan-calculator/`, `/tools/sba-guarantee-fee-calculator/` | SBA requirements, SBA bad credit, SBA women-owned businesses, SBA startup loans |
| Accounts receivable financing | `/best/accounts-receivable-financing/` | `/tools/accounts-receivable-financing-calculator/` | invoice factoring vs AR financing, AR loan requirements, revenue-based financing |
| Working capital loans | `/best/working-capital-loans/` | `/tools/business-loan-calculator/` | working capital requirements, working capital vs line of credit, short-term funding |
| Equipment financing | `/best/equipment-financing/` | `/tools/equipment-financing-calculator/` | equipment loan requirements, lease vs loan, startup equipment financing |
| Business line of credit | `/best/business-lines-of-credit/` | `/tools/business-loan-calculator/` | line of credit requirements, startup line of credit, bad-credit line of credit |
| Startup business loans | `/best/startup-business-loans/` | `/tools/business-loan-readiness-quiz/` | no revenue, no collateral, personal credit requirements, SBA startup options |
| Bad-credit personal loans | `/best/personal-loans-for-bad-credit/` | `/tools/borrowing-power-quiz/` | APR impact, approval odds, denied for credit score, secured vs unsecured |
| Secured / bad-credit cards | `/best/credit-cards-for-bad-credit/` | `/tools/credit-score-simulator/` | secured cards, credit-builder cards, approval odds, using cards before loans |

Cluster pages should link in this direction:

Supporting answer -> supporting tool -> primary money page

And also:

Primary money page -> supporting answer/tool pages

This creates a closed topical cluster instead of isolated posts.

## Priority Cluster 1: Accounts Receivable Financing

Why this is attractive:

- Low difficulty in the Lendio export.
- High CPC.
- Direct business-financing intent.
- CreditDoc appears not to have a clean primary page for it yet.

Target keywords:

- accounts receivable loan
- accounts receivable loans
- accounts receivable financing
- accounts receivable lending
- accounts receivables loan
- financing accounts receivable
- business loans based on revenue

Recommended page:

- `/best/accounts-receivable-financing/` or `/financial-wellness/accounts-receivable-financing/`

Preferred money page:

- `/best/accounts-receivable-financing/`

The `/best/` route is preferred because this is direct financing intent, not general education.

Preferred structure:

- What accounts receivable financing is
- Who qualifies
- Typical advance rates and fees
- Invoice factoring vs accounts receivable loans
- Best use cases
- Risks
- Alternatives: working capital loans, business line of credit, SBA loans
- Calculator CTA when available
- FAQ

Internal links to add:

- business loans category
- startup business loans
- working capital loans
- equipment financing
- business loan readiness quiz

- business loans for bad credit
- business loan with bad credit
- business loan bad credit

- business line of credit for startup without revenue
- startup business loans with bad credit
- business loans based on revenue

- `/best/business-loans-for-bad-credit/`

## Money Page Requirements

Money pages should not be thin listicles. Each money page should include:

- Product explanation
- Best-fit borrower profiles
- Qualification requirements
- Credit-score guidance
- Revenue/time-in-business requirements where relevant
- Cost and fee ranges
- Time to funding
- Risks and unsuitable borrowers
- Alternatives
- Comparison table
- Readiness checklist
- FAQ
- Links to tools
- Links to supporting answers

For business loan pages, include structured product alternatives:

- SBA loan
- Term loan
- Business line of credit
- Equipment financing
- Invoice factoring / accounts receivable financing
- Merchant cash advance
- Secured business loan
- Personal loan used for business, with caution

## Internal Linking Plan

Every loan-related answer page should link upward to one money page and sideways to one tool.

Example:

- Bad-credit business loan answer -> `/best/business-loans-for-bad-credit/`
- Bad-credit business loan answer -> `/tools/business-loan-readiness-quiz/`
- SBA requirements answer -> `/best/sba-loans/`
- SBA requirements answer -> `/tools/sba-loan-calculator/`
- Accounts receivable answer -> `/best/accounts-receivable-financing/`
- Accounts receivable answer -> `/tools/accounts-receivable-financing-calculator/`

Hub pages should link down to supporting answers, not just out to provider/review pages.

## Page Quality Requirements

For loan pages, avoid generic finance copy. Each page needs:

- Clear borrower fit
- Qualification requirements
- Costs/rates/fee ranges where possible
- Time-to-funding expectations
- Credit-score impact
- Documentation checklist
- Alternatives
- Risks
- FAQ with exact query phrasing
- Schema where appropriate
