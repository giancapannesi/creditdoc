# CreditDoc LinkedIn Corporate Sample Pack

Purpose: polished LinkedIn post samples for CreditDoc's 1-2 posts/week cadence. These are not live posts. Use them as approval-ready starting points for the LinkedIn draft queue.

Tone: businesslike, calm, useful, non-hype. CreditDoc should sound like a practical financial research desk, not a lender promising approvals.

## Visual Style

Use a restrained corporate look:

- White or very light background.
- Navy, charcoal, slate, and controlled CreditDoc blue accents.
- One clear headline.
- One supporting line.
- One simple chart, calculator panel, checklist, or document-style layout.
- No luxury imagery, cash piles, fake urgency, or stock-photo smiling office people.
- Avoid “guaranteed”, “instant approval”, “free money”, “get funded today”, or anything that reads like a lender ad.

Recommended formats:

- 1200x627 link-preview style image.
- 1080x1080 square image for feed.
- 1080x1350 portrait version if we later test higher mobile visibility.
