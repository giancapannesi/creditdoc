## THE BUSINESS: CREDITDOC.CO

### What It Is
A 30,000-page financial services directory targeting the 80-133M Americans with subprime or no credit. Unlike NerdWallet/Credit Karma (which only cover mainstream products that pay affiliate commissions), CreditDoc covers pawn shops, check cashing, BHPH dealers, title loans, credit repair — categories the incumbents structurally cannot serve.

### Current State (Month 2 — launched March 2026)
- **15,529 indexed lender profiles** (vs NerdWallet ~900, SuperMoney ~500)
- **9.8M CFPB consumer complaint records** integrated at company level (NO competitor does this)
- **385 CFPB enforcement actions**, 373K SBA loan records, 78K FDIC branch locations
- **51 state lending law pages**, 6 city guides (scaling to 30K at 5/day)
- **85 wellness guides, 39 blog posts, 26 answer pages, 215 comparisons, 27 money pages**
- **Revenue: $0** — no affiliate programs active yet
- **Traffic: ~26 clicks/28 days** (8,086 impressions, improving positions)
- **Tech stack: Cloudflare Workers SSR + Supabase** — $0/mo infrastructure cost
- **Team: 1 founder (Jammi) + AI agents** — zero employees

### Traffic Data (GSC)
- Site appeared in Google March 16, 2026 (less than 2 months ago)
- Impressions growing 108% over 6 weeks (3,890 → 8,086 per 28-day window)
- Average position improving: 47 → 17-22
- 91.9% of queries are navigational brand searches (people searching for specific lender names)
- Content pages (/answers/, /blog/, /wellness/) ranking positions 1-6 but with tiny impression volumes
- City guide pages ranking positions 1-5 immediately — fastest-growing page type
- 287 pages confirmed indexed (PASS), 547 pending, 16,978 being discovered

### Competitive Advantages (Confirmed Unique)
1. ONLY consumer site integrating CFPB complaint data at individual company level
2. ONLY site integrating ALL 4 federal data sources (CFPB + SBA + FDIC + state laws)
3. Categories no competitor covers (pawn, check cashing, BHPH, title loans)
4. 15,529 profiles vs competitors' hundreds
5. City-level financial guides (no competitor has these)
6. $0 infrastructure cost (Cloudflare free tier + Supabase free tier)

### What The Research Shows
**Every billion-dollar directory followed the same sequence:**
1. Data Assembly ✅ (CreditDoc: DONE)
2. Content Layer 🔄 (CreditDoc: IN PROGRESS)
3. Free Tool as Hook ❌ (CreditDoc: NOT STARTED — this is the gap)
4. UGC / Community ❌ (NOT STARTED)
5. Monetization Diversification ❌ (NOT STARTED)

**Key benchmarks from successful directories:**
- NerdWallet: $800 start → $75 year 1 → $65K year 2 → profitable by year 6 → $837M revenue
- Credit Karma: gave away free credit scores → 140M members → $8.1B acquisition
- Zillow: gave away Zestimates → 1M visitors in 48 hours → $2.6B revenue
- The Points Guy: fastest to revenue (4 months via affiliate) → $50M/year
- SuperMoney (closest competitor): $4.9M revenue with 40 people
- Median time to first meaningful revenue: 1.5-2 years
- YMYL financial sites: 6-12 month Google sandbox

**Affiliate CPA rates available NOW:**
