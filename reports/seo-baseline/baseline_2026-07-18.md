# CreditDoc SEO Baseline — 2026-07-18

**GSC data window:** 2026-06-12 → 2026-07-10 (28-day pull, latest: 2026-07-13)
**Sitemap URLs:** 27,304 (last live sweep) | **Pages in GSC data:** 4,196 (15% coverage)

## Executive read

Google IS crawling and matching CreditDoc pages against searches — but only for brand-name searches of specific lenders, and only at low positions (page 4-10). Actual transactional-intent queries barely surface at all. 84% of the site gets zero impressions.

CTR problem AND content-visibility problem, in that order.
