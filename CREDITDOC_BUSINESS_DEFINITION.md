# CreditDoc Business Definition

Status: internal canonical framing for agents and strategy work.

## Canonical Internal Description

CreditDoc is ultimately an embedded finance / AI loan-origination engine. The public directory is the wedge.

It exists to earn traffic, build trust, map lenders, gather search intent, and assemble the lender intelligence graph needed for matching and origination.

## Strategic Arc

Directory wedge -> lender intelligence graph -> AI matching engine -> embedded finance distribution -> loan origination / BrokerOS / API revenue.

## Public-Facing Short Version

CreditDoc is a consumer finance directory with aspirations to become an embedded finance company.

## Guardrail

CreditDoc is not a credit repair company.

Credit repair may appear as a content category, SEO vertical, comparison topic, affiliate vertical, or user-intent cluster. It is not the business identity and should not be used as shorthand for CreditDoc.

When describing CreditDoc, use one of these frames:

- consumer finance directory
- lender and financial product directory
- consumer finance search and comparison platform
- embedded finance / AI loan-origination engine, for internal strategy only
- lender intelligence graph and routing engine, for internal strategy only

Do not summarize CreditDoc as:

- credit repair company
- credit repair service
- credit counseling service
- debt settlement company
